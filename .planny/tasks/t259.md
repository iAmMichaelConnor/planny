Why: dragging a card from one column to another is the gesture people expect
from a kanban board, and the board now supports dragging within a column
(t248). Today a drop on another column is refused, because it would be two
acts at once — a move and a status change — and the status buttons already do
the second one.

Done looks like: a card dropped in another column changes status to that
column's status, and lands at the priority position where it was dropped. The
two changes reach the store as they do now: the status route, then the bump.
A drop on the Cancelled column is refused, because cancelling asks about
replacements and that belongs in the drawer. A drop on Parked asks for the
wake note, the same prompt the park button raises.

Open point, worth settling before building: what the operator has to confirm.
Today every priority move and every status change raises a confirm dialog, so
one drag would raise two. Decision t258 asks whether those dialogs stay; build
this after that answer, or the gesture ships twice as slow as it should feel.
