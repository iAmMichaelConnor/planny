I want the filters (from, to) to include hour:minute times too (optionally, otherwise defaulting to midnight at the start of the stated day)


Built, 2026-09-02. Each end of the window now carries an optional time beside
its date. The rules:

- A date with no time on the "from" end opens at midnight, the start of that
  day — exactly as the task asks.
- A date with no time on the "to" end closes at the last instant of that day,
  so "21 Aug to 21 Aug" still means the whole of 21 August.
- A time narrows its own end to that minute. The "to" end includes the whole
  minute named, so "to 14:30" catches something logged at 14:30:12.
- A time with no date beside it bounds nothing: there is no day to put it in.
- The presets (today, 7 days, 30 days) mean whole days, so they clear any
  typed time. Clear empties all four boxes.

One deviation from the task's wording, and why. The task says both ends
default to "midnight at the start of the stated day". Read literally, a "to"
date with no time would close the window before the day it names, so "21 Aug
to 21 Aug" would match nothing and the filter's existing behaviour would
break. The "to" end therefore keeps closing at the end of its day, which is
what "to 21 August" means in English and what the filter did before. Say the
word and it is a one-line change.

To settle the question by showing rather than telling, the filter bar now
prints the window it settled on — "21 Aug, 00:00 → 21 Aug, 23:59" — beside the
boxes, in the reader's own clock format. The rule is never a guess.
