Operator suggestion 2026-09-01: tasks that arise from a decision should refer to the decision that created them. No schema change — provenance lives in prose, both directions, as a skill rule: when a resolution spawns or reshapes tasks, each new task's body names the decision (for example 'Decided in t150'); and the record appended to the decision after the work ships names the spawned task ids. Ids are stable for the life of the store, so the citations stay greppable. Add this to the skill's Working-the-decision-queue section.
