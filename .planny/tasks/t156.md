Decision t150 resolved: build it. serve --detach re-launches the CLI as a detached child (own OS session, no setsid binary, works on macOS), with stdout and stderr appended to a predictable log in the OS temp dir; the parent waits until the child answers for this store, prints the URL, pid and log path, and exits 0. Already-serving is success (idempotent, prints the URL). A child that dies or never answers is an error: the parent reports the log tail and exits 1. serve --stop reads .planny/serve.json (the server already records port and pid), probes it, SIGTERMs a live server and waits for the port to free; a stale record is cleared; nothing running is success. Tests are end-to-end against dist/bin.js in test/detach.test.ts. After it ships, collapse the serve recipes in skills/planny/SKILL.md and README.md to the single command (t148/t149 anticipated this) and fold it into the 0.1.8 release (t153).
