The drawer's Save PATCHes the whole form, including the body captured when the form was built. If the body changes in the store while the form sits dirty (an agent appends a record, a resolve appends an Outcome), Save silently overwrites that change. t165 fixes the resolve-box path; this task is the general guard. Proposal: the form remembers the updated stamp of the task it rendered; the PATCH sends it; ops rejects the body write when the stored updated is newer, and the UI then offers to reload or overwrite. Needs an ops-level check plus a small UI conflict prompt. Red first across test/ops.test.ts, test/server.test.ts and test/ui.test.ts.
