The skill's Staying current section explains the cursor but not the consequence: the delta is delivered once, so piping a consuming catchup through head or grep destroys information you cannot re-request (only an explicit --since window recovers it). Add one caution: treat catchup output as one-shot; extract from it with a JSON tool, never truncate it; when exploring, --peek first. File: skills/planny/SKILL.md, Staying current.
