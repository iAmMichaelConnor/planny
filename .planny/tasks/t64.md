When a mutation gets the 'store is locked by another planny process' 400 — an agent held the lock past the server's 2s wait — the UI waits ~250ms and retries the request once automatically before surfacing the toast, since lock contention is transient by nature (stale locks self-break after 10s). Repeated failure still toasts, and the click stays idempotent-safe because the first attempt performed no write.
