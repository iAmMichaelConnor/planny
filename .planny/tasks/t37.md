The history log records who started a task; nothing shows or defends the claim. Scope deliberately narrow: protect the claim only — no new friction on other mutations (alarm fatigue makes reflexive flags worthless).

(a) Surface: `planny show` text and the UI drawer print created_by and 'started by <session> at <time>' (latest in-progress entry); next --json includes the holder so dispatchers skip claimed tasks.
(b) Defend the claim: `planny start` on a task held by a different team refuses, names the holder, and suggests --take; --take proceeds and logs the takeover as a history entry. An unattributed in-progress task (no by recorded) warns instead of refusing — there is no holder to name.
(c) Teams: actor ids are hierarchical — an orchestrator passes PLANNY_SESSION to subagents (env inheritance does this for free) and may suffix per child (sess-abc/builder). Ownership compares the root before the first '/': same root, no friction; history keeps per-child attribution.
(d) Soft warnings only for done/cancel on another team's in-progress task (closing a subagent's work is legitimate).
(e) Skill: use the harness session id or invent one at session start and keep it; suffix convention for subagents; ids are bookkeeping, not authentication.
