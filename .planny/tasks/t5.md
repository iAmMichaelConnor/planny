Every mutation is a read-modify-write over the whole task set. Two writers at once (the CLI and the serve UI, or two agents) can interleave and drop an update. Add an advisory lock file around Mutation.commit in src/ops.ts, held for the load-mutate-save span. Test with two concurrent ops (the Harden step of the testing philosophy calls for exactly this case).
