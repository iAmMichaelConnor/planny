The drawer defaults to the right edge. Dock buttons in its header move it to the left edge or the bottom of the page; the choice persists in localStorage. The resize handle follows the dock: it sits on the drawer's inner edge and resizes width when docked left or right, height when docked bottom. Switching dock clears any dragged size.
