## Background

Every planny mutation takes a cross-process file lock. The serve process
waits for a contended lock with a synchronous sleep, which blocks its
event loop: while it waits (up to 2 seconds), it serves nothing — reads,
other tabs, the event stream. In practice an agent holds the lock for
about 10 milliseconds per operation and the server polls every 25, so an
observed stall is typically tens of milliseconds; nobody has ever seen
one. The UI also auto-retries the timeout error once (t64), so the
failure path already self-heals.

## Why this comes to you (the operator)

The fix is roughly half a day of the most bug-prone kind of concurrency
work: ops takes the lock internally and is synchronous, so the server
cannot simply await-then-call. Async-ifying ops duplicates the mutation
surface (forbidden by this project's no-duplicate-codepaths law); a plain
reentrancy flag is unsafe across interleaved requests; the robust shape
is an in-process mutation queue plus a reentrant inner lock, with its own
contention test rig. A naive attempt would look correct and corrupt under
interleaving. You raised the concern and the effort is yours to spend —
an agent must not attempt this without your go.

## Proposal

Do not build it until a trigger fires. Triggers: someone actually
observes the board freeze for a second or more; the store grows past
roughly a thousand tasks; a second human uses the board concurrently; or
planny is hosted anywhere other than localhost.

*Pros:* no risky concurrency work for an unobserved problem; the
constraints are recorded here for whoever eventually builds it.
*Cons:* the bounded stall stays until a trigger is noticed.

## Alternative options

- Build now. Pros: robustness ahead of need. Cons: half a day of
  landmine-laden work against a symptom measured in tens of milliseconds.
- Close as never-build. Pros: lean board. Cons: the constraint analysis
  loses its natural home if conditions change.

## Needed from you

"wait for a trigger" (the proposal), "build now", or "never".

## When

No cost to waiting; answer whenever a trigger fires or the board bothers
you.
