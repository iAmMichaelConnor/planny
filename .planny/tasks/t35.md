planny's --since commands make the caller remember X, the time it last looked; an agent that loses its timestamp re-reads everything or guesses. Add `planny catchup --as <session-id> [--json]`: return every change since that consumer's stored cursor (same delta as list --changed-since plus decisions --resolved --since), then advance the cursor. Cursors live in .planny/cursors.json, keyed by consumer id; peek without advancing via --peek. Makes catch-up idiot-proof for any harness and removes the last piece of client-side state.
