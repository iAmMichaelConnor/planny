The page refreshes on change events but not when the EventSource connects or reconnects, so changes made while the server was restarting or the tab was disconnected stay invisible until a focus refresh. Refresh on the stream's open event (fires on every connect and automatic reconnect), and also on the document becoming visible — background tabs get throttled and can miss events.
