Add a parked status: work that is real, but not for now. A parked task keeps
its priority rank and still counts as work to do, so nothing is lost; it just
leaves the queue until someone wakes it.

The operator asked for this on 2026-09-02, in these words:

> Add a parked status to the kanban and to tasks (before the todo section).
> Particularly useful for decisions. Change "skip for now" to "park for now",
> and when that's clicked put it in "parked" status. Also introduce buttons to
> park in the rhs view of existing tasks, you know what i mean?

Two children carry the work: the core and the CLI first, then the board.

Done looks like: a task can be parked with an optional wake note; `planny next`
and `planny decisions` pass parked tasks over; the board shows a Parked column
before To do; the decisions tab parks instead of hiding; and the drawer parks
and wakes a task.
