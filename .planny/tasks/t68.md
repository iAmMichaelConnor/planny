## Background

The planny skill (skills/planny/SKILL.md) is about 3,500 tokens and grew
roughly 40% in one day as rules were added. Every session that invokes
the skill loads the whole body; only the ~120-token trigger description
is paid passively. Tiering means moving reference-grade detail — the
reading-the-plan recipes, the staying-current mechanics — into
references/ files an agent loads on demand, the way the decision format
already works. The body would keep only the laws and the action map.

## Why this comes to you (the operator)

This is a token-spend and ergonomics trade-off you own. Tiered, each
invocation costs less but an agent chases pointers mid-task and can skip
a reference it needed. Untiered, the skill reads in one pass but its
cost grows with every rule. The threshold for acting is a preference,
not a fact an agent can derive.

## Proposal

Adopt a threshold, act only when it is crossed: when SKILL.md passes
roughly 4,500 tokens, tier the recipes and staying-current sections into
references/ and keep the body to laws plus the action map.

*Pros:* one-pass reading for as long as it fits; a clear trigger any
agent can check with wc.
*Cons:* the growth that crosses the threshold may arrive one rule at a
time and go unnoticed until well past it.

## Alternative options

- Tier now. Pros: smallest per-invocation cost immediately. Cons:
  pointer-chasing while the skill still comfortably fits one read.
- Never tier. Pros: nothing to maintain. Cons: unbounded growth taxes
  every future invocation of every agent.

## Needed from you

"threshold" (the proposal), "tier now", or "never".

## When

No cost until the skill grows again; answer whenever it next gains rules.
