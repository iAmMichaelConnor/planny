For decision tasks which have been decided, the decision (be it text or the click of a button) should be readable below the description in the rhs pane. Is the decision recorded in a decision task's md file? 

Answer to the question in this task: yes — planny resolve appends a '## Outcome' section with the operator's response (or 'Accepted the proposal.' for the button) to the decision's body in its md file, stamps resolvedAt in the frontmatter, and logs the status change with the actor in history. Built: the drawer now renders that outcome below the description for resolved decisions — everything after the last '## Outcome' heading, later appends included, with the resolved date in the label. Files: web/app.js (outcomeOf, renderDrawer), web/style.css. Tests: three cases in test/ui.test.ts. See it: open t150 or t1 from the board.
